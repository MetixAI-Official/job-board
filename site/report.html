How we measured it

Roles come from the Metix AI Platform, which aggregates public job postings from many sources: open roles dated $window, pulled $pulled, in Bay Area cities or listed as US remote, at companies hosting events on the official SF Tech Week calendar from October 5 to 11. Companies are counted as they are named on their postings. The same title at the same company is counted once.

New means the role first appeared in the Metix AI Platform's job history inside that week. New roles reach the history within a day of their posted date, so a role counted as a repost was not just found late. LinkedIn job ids also grow over time, and they agree: none of the 361 roles at NVIDIA, 158 at Google, 89 at Adobe or 56 at Databricks carries an id as recent as that week's new roles. The history goes back about a year, so the oldest reposts may be older than shown.

Time up is the time between the first and last day a posting was seen, for $postings_closed US postings at these companies that came down between November 2025 and September 20, 2026. Gaps in collection can stretch it, and it is not time to hire.

Not included yet: week-over-week hiring pace. We cannot yet separate real changes from changes in how often each company's postings are collected.

This is Metix AI Platform analysis of its own job history, which the public API does not expose, so these figures cannot be reproduced with an API key. It is for information only, independent, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Tech Week, a16z or the companies named. Report a correction.
