    How it works

    Events and hosts come from the official SF Tech Week calendar. An event is listed when at least one of its hosts posted roles that match your filters. Universities, student groups, community and media organizers, accelerators, government offices and individuals are left out. Registration status is as of the pull.

    Roles come from the Metix AI Platform, which aggregates public job postings from many sources. This list keeps only open roles posted Sep 30 to Oct 6 (pulled Oct 7) in Bay Area cities or listed as US remote, meaning the posting names the United States but no city. Most of those are remote, but check the posting. The same title at the same company is counted once. The date shown is the one on the posting, and many roles are reposts of older openings: New marks the ones that first appeared in the Metix AI Platform's job history this week.

    Fields come from keywords in the title and can be wrong. Company labels come from public company records; a funding round is shown only where we checked it. A company missing here may still be hiring: many early-stage companies do not post on LinkedIn.

    This list is for information only and may be incomplete or out of date. Postings close and details change, so confirm the role on the employer's own posting before you apply. Nothing here is an offer of employment. It is an independent list, not affiliated with or endorsed by Tech Week, a16z or the companies named. Report a correction.

    Looking for other roles, or researching a market? The Metix AI Platform covers jobs, companies and people well beyond this list. Hiring? metix.ai finds interview-ready candidates for your open roles.
